# Market research (October 2026)

A quick web scan to check that BrightWeek is not a copy of an existing app and to find its position.
This is not legal advice or a trademark search.

## Existing apps

| App | Ages | Model | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Khan Academy Kids | 2–8 | Free | Large library, nonprofit, no weekly drops |
| PBS KIDS Games | 3–8 | Free | Adds new games weekly, but only for young kids |
| ABCmouse | 2–8 | ~$14.99/mo | 850+ lessons, fixed curriculum |
| Lingokids | 2–8 | Subscription | English and play-based learning |
| SplashLearn | 2–11 | Subscription | Math and reading, weekly parent reports |
| Pibblewick | 2–12 | £6.99/mo, £59.99/yr | Closest on range, but stops at 12 |
| Epic! | 4–12 | $13.99/mo | Digital books, not activities |
| Prodigy / Duolingo | 6+ | Freemium | Single subject (math / languages) |
| The Sprout, Printables for Kids, MontiWay | 2–10 | Subscription | Printable / PDF worksheets and activity plans |

## What makes BrightWeek different

1. **One app from age 2 to 16.** Most competitors stop at 8 or 12, so families with toddlers and
   teens need several apps. One subscription covers up to 6 kids of any age.
2. **A fresh pack every week,** themed and dated, instead of a fixed library. Kids have a reason to
   come back, and a gentle daily habit (2 free a day).
3. **Interactive on phone or tablet, not printables.** The worksheet apps above produce PDFs; BrightWeek
   is played and checked on the device, with read-aloud for pre-readers.
4. **Teen life skills** (money, probability, literary devices) alongside early-years basics.
5. **Private by design:** no ads, no tracking, profiles stay on the device.
6. **Lower price** than ABCmouse or Epic (suggested $5.99/mo or $39.99/yr).

## Originality

All activity content is written fresh for BrightWeek (see the rules in CONTENT_GUIDE.md).
Questions, wording and question sets must never be copied from other apps, worksheets or textbooks.

## Name check

"Little Brainy" (an early working name) is already used by a children's learning kit with a
companion app, so it was dropped. A search found no app called "BrightWeek" (only the Orthodox
Easter week of that name). **Before submitting, run a proper trademark search** and check both stores.
