Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026. TEMPLATE — have this reviewed before publishing.

BrightWeek Kids is designed for children aged 2 to 16 and their families. We built it to collect as little as possible.

What we collect

  • Nothing personal from children. Child profiles (a first name or nickname, an age and an avatar) and progress are stored only on the device. They are never sent to us.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking. The app contains no advertising or third-party analytics SDKs.
  • Purchases. Subscriptions are processed by Apple or Google. We use RevenueCat to verify subscription status; it receives an anonymous app user ID and purchase receipts, not names or contact details.
  • Content downloads. The app downloads weekly activity files from our content server. Like any web server, it may briefly log IP addresses for security.

Children's privacy (COPPA / GDPR-K)

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Purchases and settings are protected by a parental gate.

Deleting data

Remove a profile in the Grown-ups area, or uninstall the app, to delete all locally stored data.
